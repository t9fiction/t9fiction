## 👨‍💻 About Me

I build and operate **cloud, datacenter, and enterprise infrastructure**. My experience spans networking, systems administration, virtualization, Kubernetes, Docker, Linux, and cloud platforms. I focus on designing scalable, secure, and highly available systems across on-premises and cloud environments while continuously expanding my expertise in cloud engineering, platform engineering, automation, and modern infrastructure operations.

**Network and System Administrator** since 2009 with deep experience in enterprise networking (routing, switching, VLANs, firewalls, wireless), virtualization (Proxmox, VMware ESXi), datacenter operations, and server infrastructure. On the cloud-native side, I work extensively with AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, monitoring, and observability.

I maintain a **homelab/datacenter** for continuous learning and experimentation with emerging infrastructure technologies.

> 🎯 Actively pursuing opportunities in: **Cloud Engineering · Infrastructure Engineering · Platform Engineering · SRE · DevOps · Systems Administration · Enterprise Networking**
